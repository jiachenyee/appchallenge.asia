Nov 2026 – Jan 2027 • An initiative by Swift Students Cafe.

Applications close November 9, 2026

What?

Build an app — individually with Xcode or Swift Playground — that can be experienced in 3 minutes, without the use of internet, that demonstrates excellence in innovation, creativity, social impact, or inclusivity. The project's zip file has to be under 25 MB.

All participants will receive feedback on how they can take their projects further; Winners will receive an exclusive pin, along with special gifts.

Who?

This challenge is for students in Singapore who fulfil one of the following requirements:

  • Be enrolled in an accredited academic institution
  • Currently serving National Service
  • Graduated in the last 6 months and are awaiting acceptance or have received acceptance to an accredited academic institution.

No prior technical or design experience is required to participate.

You will need either a Mac or iPad capable of running Xcode or Swift Playground to participate.

When?

  1. Applications

    Until Nov 9
    We're looking for participants who are interested in learning something new, trying something out, and building apps! No prior experience required. Selected participants will be notified by Nov 10.

  2. Kick-Off

    Nov 14
    Join us at Apple Developer Center Singapore for a kick-off, including technical, design, and ideation sessions. During this session, you'll meet your mentor and get started on your project.
    Required for all participants.

  3. Check-Ins

    Optional check-in sessions at Apple Developer Center Singapore where you will be able to work on your projects, loan devices, and get feedback.

  4. Submission

    Jan 9 – Jan 13
    Submit your creations online during the submission window.

  5. Celebration

    Jan 23
    All participants are invited to join us for a special half-day celebration at Apple Developer Center Singapore to celebrate the work.
    Winners will be announced at this event.
